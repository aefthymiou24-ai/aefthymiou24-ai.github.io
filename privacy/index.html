Privacy Policy

Quantum Codex for iPhone and iPad

Last updated 22 November 2026.

Quantum Codex is a game made by Alex Efthymiou ("we", "us"). This page explains what information the game uses, who else is involved, and the choices you have. If you have a question, email aefthymiou24@icloud.com.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, and we don't run any servers. We don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your device only.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some information from your device to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • The game checks the time over the internet now and then, with Google's or Apple's web servers.

What stays on your device

The game saves the following on your iPhone or iPad. None of it is sent to us.

  • Your game progress: your pals, Quantum Coins, upgrades and codex.
  • Your settings: sound effects, music and haptics.
  • Which Premium Shop items you own, so they keep working offline.
  • How long you've played, so full-screen ads don't appear in your first minutes of play.

This information can be included in your own iCloud or computer backups. Deleting the app deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Quantum Codex shows ads provided by Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Most are ads you choose to watch for an in-game reward; a full-screen ad also appears after about every 5 minutes of play. Buying Remove Ads stops the full-screen ads.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect the following from your device. According to Google's disclosure, this can include:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking, see below) or other identifiers tied to this app or developer.
  • Coarse location, such as a location estimated from your IP address.
  • Advertising data, such as the ads you've seen and how you interacted with them.
  • Other interactions, such as app launches and video views.
  • Diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance data.

Google uses this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The game asks for ads suitable for general audiences only.

Tracking and personalised ads

Before the first ad, the game shows Apple's question asking whether Quantum Codex may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, Google can't use your device's advertising identifier, and you'll still see ads, which may be less relevant. You can change your answer at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

In some regions, such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you'll also be shown Google's consent message, where you can choose how your information is used for ads.

Purchases (Apple)

Items in the Premium Shop are bought through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and its own privacy policy applies: Apple Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment details. The game is only told which items your Apple ID owns, so it can unlock them. RESTORE PURCHASES in the game's Settings asks Apple for them again.

The purchases are handled inside the game by Unity's In-App Purchasing software, which talks to Apple directly. The game doesn't send your purchases anywhere else.

The time check

So time-based progress stays fair, the game asks a web server for the current time: Google's (www.google.com) or, as a backup, Apple's (www.apple.com). The request contains no information about you or your game. As with any internet connection, those servers can see your IP address.

Children

Quantum Codex is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Your choices

  • Turn tracking off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Manage how Google personalises ads at My Ad Center.
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop full-screen ads.
  • Delete the app to delete everything the game saved on your device.
  • Email us with any question or request about your information. Because we don't hold any of your personal information ourselves, requests about data collected for ads are best made to Google using the links above.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information differently, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
